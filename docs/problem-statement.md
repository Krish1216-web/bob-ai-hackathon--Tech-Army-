# 🎯 Problem Statement — ChainGuard AI

## Overview
Global enterprise supply chains and domestic logistics corridors operate under unprecedented friction. Disruptions ranging from port strikes, severe climatic events, and highway blockages to unexpected carrier capacity reductions cause severe cascading delays and billions in financial loss.

## The Core Pain Points

### 1. Lack of Unified Disruption Visibility
Supply chain dispatchers currently operate with siloed visibility. When a major node (such as JNPT / Mumbai Port) faces an operational bottleneck, dispatchers only discover delays after shipments miss critical milestones.

### 2. High Fleet Idleness & Asymmetric Capacity
Up to 30% of specialized reefer fleets operate at sub-20% capacity utilisation due to lack of intelligent matchmaking between idle assets and delayed high-demand corridors.

### 3. Critical Cold-Chain Spoilage (Pharma & Perishables)
Pharmaceutical payloads (e.g. mRNA vaccines requiring strict $2^\circ\text{C}\text{--}8^\circ\text{C}$ SOP bounds) suffer irreversible degradation when temperature excursions exceed thermal limits. Existing systems offer passive alarms without real-time dynamic spoilage projections or automated diversion to qualified cold-storage facilities.

### 4. Absence of Closed-Loop Decision Execution
Even when AI models generate recommendations, dispatchers must manually transcribe actions across multiple legacy ERP systems, delaying response times by 12–24 hours and compounding financial exposure.

---

## Target Users & Impact
- **Supply Chain Directors & Control Tower Dispatchers**: Gain single-pane-of-glass operational awareness and 1-click execution.
- **Fleet & Asset Managers**: Automatically reallocate idle reefers to revenue-generating corridors.
- **Quality & Cold-Chain Compliance Officers**: Prevent spoilage with certified cold-storage hub diversions.
